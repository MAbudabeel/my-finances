                Backup

                Your data lives only on this phone, inside this app. If you delete the app from the Home Screen, clear Safari's website data, or lose or change your phone, it's gone unless you have a backup.

                Tap Back up → Save backup file → Save to Files → iCloud Drive. The file is always called My Finances backup.json, so choose Replace and you keep one up-to-date copy. It's encrypted with your password: nobody can read it without the password, including Apple. Face ID and the PIN don't open backups.

                An iPhone web app isn't allowed to save files by itself, so this takes two taps. A reminder appears under Add bank texts when there's something new to back up.

                Al Rajhi texts

                Your iPhone automation saves each Al Rajhi text to bank-texts.txt and copies that file. When you unlock this app, it reads the copied texts (tap Paste when iOS asks) and adds the new ones. Texts already added are skipped.

                Missed one? Copy a text in Messages (press and hold → Copy) and tap Paste texts on the Overview.

                Wallet and card texts

                A separate iPhone automation (one per wallet, tagged like ##WALLET:STCPAY##) saves those texts to wallet-texts.txt in On My iPhone › Finances and copies them together with your Al Rajhi texts. The Al Rajhi automation is not changed.

                After the app has added them, your next tap in the app puts a short “done” note on the clipboard. The next wallet text then empties wallet-texts.txt first, so texts don't pile up in a readable file.

                Security

                It also locks when you leave the app for more than a minute.
